Object Deformability Score Calculation

The Object Deformability Score (QO) is a pre-grasp attribute used in the GRAB benchmark to characterize object deformation and its influence on robotic graspability.

1. Object Deformability as a Graspability Attribute

In the GRAB benchmark, the Object Deformability Score (QO) characterizes the intrinsic deformation behaviour of individual objects and is determined prior to grasp trial execution.

The benchmark incorporates 21 distinct objects, comprising three representative items from each of seven inorganic contaminant categories. All objects weigh less than 100 g and fit within a bounding volume of 20 × 10 × 10 cm³, ensuring compatibility with the gripper payload, opening width, and suction constraints.

Unlike conventional object datasets primarily consisting of rigid objects, the GRAB dataset incorporates both rigid and deformable objects, representing a broad range of geometric and deformation characteristics.

Since object weight and dimensions were constrained to satisfy the grippers' operational requirements, deformability was selected as the primary object-related property for evaluating graspability.

Accordingly, object deformation is parameterized as the Object Deformability Score (QO), representing the object-related pre-grasp condition within the GRAB benchmark.
